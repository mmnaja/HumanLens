About us

About HumanLens IT Solutions

The technology division of HumanLens (Pvt) Ltd, a media and creative services company based in Galle, Sri Lanka.

Our background

A media company that expanded into technology

HumanLens (Pvt) Ltd is an established media and creative services company in Sri Lanka, with a track record in media production, content creation and digital storytelling.

We launched HumanLens IT Solutions as a division to bring that experience into technology: web development, mobile applications, custom software and IT consulting for businesses pursuing digital transformation.

The result is a team that combines creative insight with technical delivery, backed by the credibility of HumanLens Media.

Why we exist

Locally managed IT that meets international standards

Demand for high-quality, locally managed IT services is growing in Sri Lanka. Many companies still struggle with fragmented vendor support, delayed delivery and tools that do not match how they operate. We close that gap with responsive, context-aware and scalable digital solutions.
