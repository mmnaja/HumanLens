FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is your typical project timeline?

Timelines depend on scope. We agree on milestones and a delivery date in writing before work starts.

Do you offer ongoing support and maintenance?

Yes. Our maintenance and hosting service covers web hosting, updates and system monitoring on a monthly or annual subscription.

What technologies do you work with?

Our recent projects use React and Tailwind CSS on the front end, and Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL and JWT authentication on the back end. We choose the stack for each project based on its needs.

How do you ensure project quality?

We follow structured quality assurance: code reviews, automated testing, user acceptance testing and continuous integration. Quality is built into each stage of development.

Do you work with clients outside Sri Lanka?

Yes. Our team is based in Sri Lanka and works with clients remotely, sharing progress through cloud-based project management tools.
